# Audit corrective actions

A finalized exception may have one owned corrective action. In **Audit management**, an analyst assigns it to their own workspace account, records the action and a UTC due date, and checks whether it is scheduled, due or overdue. The original audit and its hash are not changed.

In **Risk and controls**, record a new passing test on the exact audited control with a fictional reference, then have another account accept it. A passing test that predates the action, belongs to another control, or has been superseded cannot be used. A material action edit requires a retest against the edited plan. This workbench does not accept external vendor evidence or documents in this corrective workflow.

Submit the precise test as an evidence attempt. A reviewer other than the owner can return it or close it with rationale. A newer test appearing before closure blocks closure. A returned attempt requires fresh retest evidence. Approval freezes the finding, plan, owner, submission, test and decision in a downloadable JSON snapshot and includes a SHA-256 checksum (not a signature).

An independently accepted failing retest recorded after closure can reopen the action. Approved historic closure snapshots remain available. Choosing review, reopening or closure does not send notices or automatically revise any other audit or risk assessment. Direct owner delegation and escalation are not part of this local synthetic slice.
